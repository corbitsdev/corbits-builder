# Build a web app from an idea

This ready-made plan takes an idea to a working app through four plugin actions: writer write-requirements, designer mockup, writer write-build-plan and coder build. Add it to the plan with plan({ operation: "add", plan: "build-web-app" }), which also has the project follow it; once the app exists, offer the optional last step, writer write of a user manual, and drop it if the person declines. It is advice about the order, not a gate. Each step lists what the plan suggests having first; when that is missing, execute still starts the step and its result says what the plan suggested and why. Only the person's go-ahead holds a step back: while they decide approvals themselves, a step that asks for their approval of a document or for the design they chose waits for it.

Start with the requirements. Talk the idea through with the person until you know who it is for and what it must do, then start writer write-requirements with what they said. Ask only what would change what gets built and only they can decide, together in one question call; decide the rest as assumptions they can correct. When the requirements arrive, read them, put the open questions that are theirs to the person, and revise the requirements with their answers. Keep revising as they answer; the requirements are the record of what they want.

The person decides what the app does, how it looks and when it is built. They approve the requirements before the design starts and the build plan before the build. Approvals follow how they chose to work: when their preferences leave approvals to you, a filed document counts as approved and you tell them about it instead of asking. Otherwise put the documents in front of them with the writer's approve screen, question({ plugin: "writer", action: "approve", input: { kinds } }), once they have no open questions left; their answer on it records the approval.

For the design, ask whether they want one design you suggest or a few looks to compare, and what ideas they have for the look. They choose a design themselves from the options designer mockup files, and the designer's report says which screen shows them. When they send comments instead of approving, start designer mockup again with them. The build plan is written from the requirements and the chosen design, and the build starts from both.

Work that does not depend on other work can run at once, such as a document being written while the designer tries looks.

The plan ends when the app exists. After that, a change the person asks for is coder change, with nothing from this plan in the way.

When the person tells you to just build it, every step still runs, in order: coder build needs the filed requirements or build plan, because the specialists work from the documents. While they decide approvals themselves, open the project plugin's hand-over screen once, question({ plugin: "project", action: "hand-over", input: { decides: "person" } }); when they hand approvals over, start each step as the one before it files, without asking. Leave the plan with execute({ plugin: "build-web-app", input: { leave: true } }) when they direct the work elsewhere, such as only a mockup.
