# Animated Weather: User Manual

Animated Weather shows you the weather where you are, or in any city you choose, with the whole
screen moving to match it. Rain falls, snow drifts, storms flash, wind blows the clouds, heat
shimmers and fog rolls in, by day and by night. Under the scene are the tools you use every day:
your location, a city search, your saved cities, a choice of degrees, and a forecast for the next
24 hours and the next 7 days. You can also compare two to four cities side by side.

Nothing you do here needs an account. Your saved cities and settings stay in your own browser.

## Your weather right now

![The home page showing the current weather for your location](manual/home.png)

The first time you open the app, your browser asks whether it may use your location. If you allow
it, the home page shows the weather where you are: the temperature, what the sky is doing, and how
it feels. Below that is the forecast for the next 24 hours and for the next 7 days.

If you would rather not share your location, choose not to. The app then takes you to the search
page so you can pick a city instead.

The scene behind the numbers changes with the weather. On a clear night you see stars; in a storm
you see lightning. If the animation is too much for your device, you can turn on simpler effects
on the Settings page.

## Finding a city

![The search page with a city typed into the search box](manual/search.png)

Open **Search** from the menu, then type the name of a city. Matching places appear as you type.
Choose one to see its weather on the home page. Use the star on a city to keep it in your saved
cities.

## Your saved cities

![The cities page listing the saved cities](manual/cities.png)

**Cities** lists every place you have saved, each with its current weather at a glance. Choose a
city to open its full forecast. Use the star again to remove a city from the list.

## Comparing cities

![The compare page with several cities side by side](manual/compare.png)

**Compare** puts two, three or four of your cities next to each other, so you can see at once
which is warmer, wetter or windier. Pick the cities from your saved list. The app remembers your
picks for next time.

## Settings

![The settings page with the degrees choice and the simpler effects switch](manual/settings.png)

On **Settings** you can:

- Choose **Celsius** or **Fahrenheit** for every temperature in the app.
- Turn on **Simpler effects** if the moving scene makes your device slow or warm.
- Add the app to your phone or computer's home screen, so it opens like any other app.

## Good to know

- The weather comes from a free public weather service and refreshes each time you open a page.
- Your saved cities, your choice of degrees and your compare picks are kept in this browser only.
  Clearing your browser's data removes them.
- If the app cannot reach the weather service, it tells you so and shows the last weather it saw
  for that place.
