# Product Requirements: Field Service Dispatch Board

## Screens & Navigation

1. **Dispatch Queue (`/`)**:
   - **KPI & SLA Strip**: Open work orders, technicians on site, SLA at-risk count, and median response time.
   - **Filterable Work Order Table**: Work order ID, customer site, priority badge, required skill, assigned technician, status badge, and SLA timer.
   - **Assignment & Status Detail Panel**: Customer contact, equipment serial, status progression buttons, and recommended technicians ranked by skill match and current load.
2. **Technicians Roster (`/technicians`)**:
   - Shift status (`Available`, `On Job`, `Off Shift`), certifications, active work order, and completed jobs today.
