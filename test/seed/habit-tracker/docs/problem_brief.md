# Problem Brief: Daily Streak & Habit Tracker

## Problem Statement

People who want to build 3 to 7 daily habits abandon complex productivity suites because logging a check-in takes too many taps or buries today's progress behind calendars, social feeds, and gamified clutter.

## Target User

An individual tracking personal daily routines (hydration, reading, stretching, language practice) who opens the app morning and evening for under 30 seconds.

## Core Loop

1. See today's date, overall completion count, and all active habits on a single screen.
2. Tap a habit checkbox to record or undo today's completion immediately.
3. Watch the current streak, best streak, and 7-day rolling history update in place.
4. Add or edit a habit with a title, target cadence, and optional local reminder time.

## Scope Boundaries

- Single-user workspace with SQLite persistence.
- No social leaderboards, coaching feeds, or paid subscription tiers.
