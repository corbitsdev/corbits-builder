# Problem brief exemplar

A compact example of the shape and tone, for a small gym's class booking
app. Match the shape; take every fact from the project's own documents.

```markdown
## In short

- **Class booking for one climbing gym**, replacing the paper sign-up sheet
  at the front desk.
- **Members book and cancel from their phones**; staff see each class roster
  and mark who came.
- **Payments stay in the gym's existing till** for the first version.
- Success is **no more double-booked classes** and rosters ready before
  each class starts.

## Problem statement

The gym runs twelve classes a week from a paper sheet at the front desk
(your message). Members can only book in person, places get double-counted,
and staff rebuild each roster by hand before class.

## Who is affected

- Members, who book classes and want to know a place is theirs.
- Front-desk staff, who take bookings and check people in.
- The owner, who decides how many classes to run.

## What happens today

Members write their name on the sheet; staff copy names into a spreadsheet
each evening. A crossed-out name often frees a place nobody sees.

## What a fix would be worth

About three staff hours a week of copying, and the classes you said run
half-empty while members are turned away.

## Success criteria

- A member books a class from a phone and sees it in their bookings.
- A full class accepts no further bookings.
- Staff open a class and see its roster with no copying.

## Assumptions

- One gym location; more locations wait for a later version.
- Members sign in with an email address.

## Open questions

1. Should members pay when they book, or keep paying at the desk?
```
